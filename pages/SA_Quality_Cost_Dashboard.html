Source and calculation notes

Source: Copy of Aug-Quality cost reduction performance-SA_2026.xlsx · Each factory (data). Embedded snapshot: January–August 2026. Values are recalculated from monthly department cost amounts, not workbook average columns. 1 K-Yen = JPY 1,000. Customer claim cost is excluded. Department names follow source categories; similar labels across plants do not imply identical products or processes. Plant rankings compare absolute cost amounts and are not efficiency rankings. IPSG has no reported sorting cost; this is shown as N/R, not zero. Months after August are not available. The source summary has older year labels; this dashboard uses the 2026 factory detail headings. This version contains the supplied report snapshot; it does not import new Excel files.
